# Enemy list

## Fast knight

### Stats

- HP: 60
- Atk: 1
- Blk: 2

### Actions

- Battle stance: Lose 3 block, gain 3 attack
- Defensive stance: Gain 3 block, lose 3 attack

Action cycle:

1. Battle stance
2. Defensive stance
3. Battle stance
4. Defensive stance

## Slow knight

- HP: 60
- Atk: 1
- Blk: 2

### Actions

- Battle stance: Lose 3 block, gain 3 attack
- Defensive stance: Gain 3 block, lose 3 attack

Action cycle:

1. Battle stance
2. None
3. Defensive stance
4. None

## Barbarian

### Stats

- HP: 60
- Atk: 1
- Blk: 0

### Actions

- Blood rage: Deal 1 damage to self every turn.

Action cycle:

1. Blood rage

### Passive abilities

- Barbarian's rage: Gains 1 attack each time it takes damage.
