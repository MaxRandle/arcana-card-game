# Card keywords

Dictionary definitions for card effect keywords & terminology.

---

### Ramp x

Increases the cost of the card by "x" mana until the end of combat each time it is played. This can be a negative number.

Duplicates of the same card ramp independently. A card's effective cost floors at 0 (never negative); cost resets to base at end of combat.

### Permanent

The effect survives the combat-end reset and persists for the rest of the run.
