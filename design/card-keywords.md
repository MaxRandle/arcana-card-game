# Card keywords

Dictionary definitions for card effect keywords & terminology.

---

Ramp x - Increases the cost of the card by "x" mana until the end of combat each time it is played. This can be a negative number.

Fleeting x - The described effect expires at the start of the players turn in "x" turns from when the card is played. If the "x" term is omitted, the default x value is 1.

Permanent - The effect of the card persists after combat ends.
