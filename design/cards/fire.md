# Fire cards

## Cinders

- Mana: 1
- Target: Enemy
- Effect: Inflicts 1 stack of burn;

## Conflagration

- Mana: 2
- Target: N/A
- Effect: Each burning enemy seeds another random enemy with 1 stack of burn;

## Purging flame

- Mana: 1
- Target: Enemy
- Effect: Deal 3 damage; Remove 1 random buff from the target;

## Vital energy

- Mana: 0
- Target: N/A
- Effect: Lose 1 hp; gain 1 mana;
