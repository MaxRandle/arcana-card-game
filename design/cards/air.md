# Air cards

## Windshear

- Mana: 1
- Target: Enemy
- Effect: Deal 3 damage

## Twinkletoes

- Mana: 3
- Target: Player
- Effect: Gain the Twinkletoes buff

## Tenacity

- Mana: 1
- Target: Player
- Effect: Remove all debuffs

## Spirit Energy

- Mana: 1
- Target: Player
- Effect: Increases elemental damage you deal by 1
