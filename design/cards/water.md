# Water cards

## Ebb & flow

- Mana: 1
- Target: Player or Enemy
- Effect: Deal 3 damage to an enemy, or heal 3 hp to your arcanist

## Splash

- Mana: 2
- Target: Enemy
- Effect: Deal 4 damage to the target, deal 2 damage to all other enemies

## Temporal energy

- Mana: 3
- Target: N/A
- Effect: Ramp 2; Gain 1 additional mana per turn;
