# Water cards

## Water whip

- Mana: 1
- Target: Player or Enemy
- Effect: Deals 3 damage if cast on an enemy, or heals 3 if cast on a player

## Renewing droplets

- Mana: 2
- Target: Player
- Effect: For the rest of combat, you heal 2 at the start of each turn

## Splash

- Mana: 2
- Target: Enemy
- Effect: Deals 4 damage to primary target, 2 less damage to all other enemies.
